# SunWatch logo

Created September 27, 2026 with the built-in image generation tool.

Current asset (repository root): `public/sunwatch-logo-tactical.png`, 2172 × 724 pixels. Used in the original globe HUD header, startup screen and favicon. The earlier navy logo remains under `sunwatch/public/` as design history.

Design brief: a warm golden sun combined with a clean radar/orbit motif, paired with a navy SunWatch wordmark, for a calm and credible hurricane/branch situational-awareness dashboard. The mark is independent of Suncoast's official brand.

The tactical edition uses a cyan orbit, amber sun and luminous white/cyan wordmark on uniform navy. It was generated with the built-in image tool, then refined using this final edit prompt:

> Edit this SunWatch logo. Preserve the luminous cyan orbital sun mark, amber sun and white/cyan SUNWATCH wordmark exactly in spirit. Remove EVERY bit of gray checkerboard and replace the whole background with a perfectly uniform very dark navy-black #05090f. No checkerboard, no transparency grid, no texture, no panel, no shadow cast on a surface. Keep the bright logo crisp with restrained cyan glow against the flat dark background. Same horizontal composition, suitable for a tactical geospatial HUD header. Output final usable branding asset.
